HCFA Claim — Business Rules

hcfa.py — hcfa_data_entry()

What the macro does to an HCFA claim in business terms — no screen coordinates, no PF-key mechanics. Just: which rule setting fires, what it means for the claim, and what stops the claim short of release.

Framing: hcfa.py does not decide DENY vs. PAID — the rule (deny_clm, denial_code) already made that call before this runs. hcfa.py's job is to write that decision onto the claim's service lines in the way the payer's system expects, apply every other per-rule adjustment, and attempt to release the claim. If any check along the way can't be satisfied, it stops and hands the claim back for manual review instead of releasing it.
